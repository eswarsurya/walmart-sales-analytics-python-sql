# Walmart Sales Analytics — Python & SQL

**Status: Completed analytics project**

Retail analytics project using Python, SQL, and business reporting to explore Walmart weekly sales performance, store-level trends, holiday behaviour, and external business factors.

## Business Questions

- Which stores generated the highest total weekly sales?
- How did sales change across 2010, 2011, and 2012?
- Do holiday weeks perform differently from non-holiday weeks?
- How do temperature, fuel price, CPI, and unemployment relate to weekly sales?

## Tools Used

Python, Pandas, SQL, Jupyter Notebook, Excel, PowerPoint, and business analytics reporting.

## What I Implemented

- Prepared and explored store-level weekly sales data.
- Built Python-based summary outputs.
- Wrote Walmart-specific SQL analysis queries.
- Produced public-safe evidence and aggregate visuals.
- Documented business interpretation and limitations.

## Key Findings

- **Store 20** is the leading store by reviewed total weekly sales.
- **2011** records the highest total sales in the dataset.
- Holiday weeks show higher average weekly sales than non-holiday weeks.
- **2012 is a partial year** in the available data and ends in October.
